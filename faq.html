📚 Books and Shopping

Book Bazaar is a student-focused book marketplace project where users can explore books, manage a wishlist, add books to a cart, and discover student learning tools.
Open the Books section on the home page. You can browse the available collection and use the search and category options if they are available on your page.
Click the Add to Cart button on a book. Open the Cart page to review the items saved by your browser.
Add a book to your wishlist to keep track of books you may want to read or purchase later. Your wishlist is stored in your browser.

🔄 Book Exchange

The Book Exchange page lets you create sample listings for books you want to exchange. Contacting another user and completing a real exchange may require arrangements outside this demo website.
In this version, listings are stored in your browser. They are not automatically shared with other users or devices.

🎓 Student Features

Student Tools includes a book recommendation quiz, a study timer, a reading progress list, and a student budget calculator.
The deals currently displayed are sample prices for this student project. They are not verified offers from real sellers.
The current Book Assistant uses predefined recommendations and keyword matching. It does not connect to a real AI service.

🔐 Accounts and Privacy

You can browse the demo pages without a real online account. Registration and login in this version use browser storage only.
This version stores certain information in your browser using localStorage. It does not provide a shared online database. Avoid entering real passwords or sensitive personal information.
